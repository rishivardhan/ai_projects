# Objection Handling Guide

## "Your price is too high"
Acknowledge, then reframe around total cost of ownership. Ask what the budget number is based on. Offer options in this order: reduce scope (fewer add-ons in year one), multi-year commitment discount, phased rollout. Avoid leading with a discount. Reference the Pricing and Discount Approval Policy for approval thresholds.

## "We already use Snowflake / Databricks"
Position Northwind Cloud as the governed analytics and AI layer that can sit next to an existing warehouse via the Data Connectors Pack. Emphasize AI Assist natural-language querying and built-in dashboards, which those tools require third-party BI for.

## "We need to build it in-house"
Quantify the engineering cost: a typical in-house stack requires 3-5 data engineers to maintain. Share the time-to-value benchmark of 6 weeks median.

## "Security and compliance concerns"
Northwind Cloud is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified, HIPAA eligible (BAA available), and GDPR compliant. EU data residency is available in Frankfurt and Dublin regions. Customer-managed encryption keys (BYOK) are supported on the Core Platform for Enterprise and Strategic tiers. Send the security questionnaire pack and SOC 2 report under NDA.

## "The timing is not right"
Agree on a specific re-engagement date, keep the champion warm with relevant content, and log a follow-up task. Ask whether a smaller pilot could proceed in the current quarter.
