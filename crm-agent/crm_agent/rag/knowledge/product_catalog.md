# Northwind Cloud Product Catalog

## Core Platform
The Core Platform is Northwind Cloud's managed cloud data platform: storage, a columnar query engine, governance and role-based access control. List price is $48,000 per year for the base package, which includes 50 named users and 10 TB of managed storage. Additional users are $600 per user per year. Additional storage is $1,800 per TB per year. The Core Platform supports SSO via SAML 2.0 and OIDC (Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace), row-level security, column masking and full audit logging.

## Analytics Add-on
The Analytics Add-on adds self-service dashboards, scheduled reports, embedded analytics and aggregation caching for very large tables. List price is $18,000 per year. It requires the Core Platform. Aggregation caching is the recommended fix for dashboards over tables with more than 500 million rows.

## AI Assist
AI Assist lets business users ask questions in natural language and get governed SQL, charts and narrative summaries. It respects row-level security and never trains on customer data. List price is $24,000 per year. It requires the Core Platform. AI Assist is the strongest upsell motion for customers with growing analyst headcount.

## Data Connectors Pack
The Data Connectors Pack includes 150+ managed connectors (Salesforce, SAP, NetSuite, Workday, Oracle, Postgres, Kafka streaming and more) with change-data-capture. List price is $9,000 per year. Custom connectors for on-premise databases are available through Professional Services at $15,000 per connector, typically delivered in 4-6 weeks.

## Premium Support
Premium Support provides a named technical account manager, 1-hour response for Severity 1 issues, 24x7 coverage and quarterly architecture reviews. List price is $12,000 per year or 10% of total annual contract value, whichever is greater. Standard support (included with every subscription) offers business-hours coverage and 8-hour response for Severity 1.
