# Pricing and Discount Approval Policy

## Discount authority
Account executives may approve discounts up to 10% off list price without additional approval. Discounts from 10% up to 20% require approval from the Regional Sales Director. Discounts above 20% and up to 30% require approval from the VP of Sales and the Deal Desk. Discounts above 30% are not permitted except for strategic logos approved by the CRO.

## Multi-year commitments
A two-year prepaid commitment qualifies for an additional 5% discount. A three-year commitment qualifies for an additional 8% discount. Multi-year discounts stack with the rep-level discount but count toward the total approval threshold.

## Bundles and concessions
Premium Support may be bundled at no charge for the first year only on deals above $100,000 in annual contract value, with Regional Sales Director approval. Free pilots are limited to 30 days and require a written success plan with measurable criteria.

## Competitive situations
When a competitor offers a lower price, lead with total cost of ownership: Northwind Cloud customers report 30-45% lower infrastructure and admin costs versus self-managed stacks, and a median time-to-value of 6 weeks. Do not match competitor pricing below the 20% threshold without Deal Desk review. Request the competitive quote in writing.

## Payment terms
Standard payment terms are annual in advance, net 30. Quarterly billing is available for contracts above $150,000 with a 3% uplift.
