# Support Policy and SLAs

## Severity definitions
Severity 1: production down or data unavailable for all users. Severity 2: major feature impaired, workaround difficult (for example scheduled reports failing). Severity 3: minor issue or question. Severity 4: feature request.

## Response targets
Standard support: Severity 1 in 8 business hours, Severity 2 in 1 business day, Severity 3 in 2 business days.
Premium Support: Severity 1 in 1 hour 24x7, Severity 2 in 4 hours, Severity 3 in 1 business day, with a named technical account manager.

## Escalation
Account owners can escalate any ticket open longer than its response target through the #support-escalations channel. Customers with negative sentiment on two or more recent tickets should be flagged to Customer Success for a health check, and the health score should be reviewed before renewal.

## Renewals
Renewal conversations start 120 days before the renewal date. Customers with a health score under 60 within 90 days of renewal are considered at risk and require an executive sponsor touchpoint.
