# Sales Playbook

## Opportunity stages and exit criteria
Prospecting: a target account has been identified and initial outreach made. Exit when a discovery call is scheduled.
Qualification: discovery call held. Exit criteria use MEDDICC: identified Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Identified pain, Champion and Competition. Probability 20%.
Proposal: solution presented, pricing proposal sent. Requires a completed technical validation. Probability 50%.
Negotiation: commercial and legal terms in discussion, MSA redlines in progress, verbal selection. Probability 75%.
Closed Won: order form signed. Closed Lost: record a loss reason.

## Deal hygiene rules
Every open opportunity must have a next step and a close date in the future. An opportunity with a close date in the past must be updated within 2 business days. Deals with no logged interaction in the last 21 days are considered stalled and must be reviewed in the weekly pipeline call.

## Handling a lost champion
If the champion leaves the company, immediately identify a new sponsor at VP level or above, request an introduction from the departing champion, and re-validate the decision criteria. Treat the deal as at-risk and lower probability by one stage.

## Expansion motions
Customers with a health score above 75 and growing usage are prime candidates for AI Assist and additional Core Platform seats. Run a quarterly business review before proposing expansion. Customers with health score below 50 need a success plan before any upsell conversation.

## Lead qualification
Leads with a score of 70 or higher and a confirmed project are marked Qualified and should be converted to an account, contact and opportunity within 5 business days. Leads under 30 are Unqualified unless they come from a referral. Respond to inbound web form leads within 24 hours.
